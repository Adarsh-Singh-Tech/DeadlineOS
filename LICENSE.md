## Why Apache 2.0 for DeadlineOS

Apache 2.0 is the strongest professional default for a startup-grade, cloud-native SaaS engineering repository. It was chosen over MIT, custom, and proprietary options for these reasons:

| Comparison | Apache 2.0 Advantage |
|---|---|
| **vs MIT** | Adds an express patent grant and clearer redistribution conditions — critical for product-oriented, commercially sensitive codebases |
| **vs Custom Portfolio License** | Standard licenses reduce legal friction, increase ecosystem familiarity, and signal engineering credibility to recruiters and contributors |
| **vs Proprietary** | Enables public showcase value and developer trust while preserving attribution and ownership clarity |

**The founder positioning:** Apache 2.0 provides open enough visibility for portfolio and technical review, with structured enough governance for future productization — the right balance for an AI-native SaaS repository.

## Founder Positioning Note

If this public repository is intended to showcase engineering quality while a future commercial product — including hosted execution, proprietary workflows, managed services, customer data, and production infrastructure — remains independently controlled, Apache 2.0 works well for this separation.

This is particularly suited where:

- The repository is a **product engineering case study**
- The SaaS business model depends on **hosted execution** rather than raw source exclusivity
- Brand, customer workflows, datasets, and deployment systems remain **outside the public repo scope**
- Future enterprise features may be **commercialized separately**

If the goal later shifts to strict commercialization control over the repository itself, a source-available or proprietary model can be adopted for future versions.
